The NextBSD desktop, redesigned

The proposed theme takes the dark NextBSD palette and the cyan accent. It drops the NeXT bevels for soft, rounded controls with thin borders, and title bars that quiet down when a window isn’t active. Everything below is live. Click a window to make it active, drag it by its title bar, and use the controls. Switch to Today’s theme to compare.

What changes in nextbsd-theme

The colours stay in Tools/make-colors.m, with the same names and a lighter selection (#1b4a63). The bevels and square corners come from GSTheme’s default drawing, so the new look needs NextBSD.m to draw buttons, fields, popups, checkboxes, radios, switches, segments, title bars and scrollers itself: a rounded, filled shape with a 1-point border, the accent on default buttons and checked controls, and the inactive variants (grey selection, neutral default button, dim title) keyed on the window’s key state. The window buttons stay where NextBSD has them: miniaturize on the left, close on the right.
